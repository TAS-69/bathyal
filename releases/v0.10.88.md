## ◆ &nbsp;Installing

Import the `.mrpack` into any launcher that reads the Modrinth format — **Prism Launcher**
(*Add Instance → Import → Modrinth pack*), the **Modrinth App** (*Import → From file*), or **ATLauncher**.

The first import needs an internet connection and a few minutes: **105 of the 109 mods are downloaded from their
authors' own pages** at that point rather than being carried in the file. The first world load is also slow — the
terrain is read from image data rather than generated from noise.

**8 GB** allocated is a sensible starting point. **Distant Horizons ships at a 64-chunk radius** and is by a wide
margin the heaviest thing in the pack; turn it down first if you struggle.

## ◆ &nbsp;What the pack itself adds

Everything below is this project's own code and content, not a mod someone else wrote:

- **A layered map-driven world** — a custom generator combines terrain, biome, climate and coastline data;
  ~12.3 km across, sea at Y132.
- **40 settlements** with culture-specific architecture, furnished interiors, walls, gatehouses,
  farmland and **129 roads**; **21 harbours** with working port buildings ashore.
- **Six playable races** with traits, abilities and their own learning rates, chosen at character creation.
- **26 skill trees** levelled by doing the work, with exclusive forks so two players in a trade diverge.
- **Three schools of magic**, studied rather than bought, with their own grimoires and arts.
- **Standing in three layers** — faction, settlement and personal — driving prices, greetings and available work.
- **335 quests**, conversation that varies by faction, mood and race, and local rumours.
- **A currency, shops with daily stock, and private trade** with any resident.
- **Crime and consequence** — stealth, theft, witnesses, the watch, fines and cells.
- **Ships bought from shipwrights**, crews who keep names, levels and loyalty, liveries, figureheads, and a sea
  that runs port-to-port trade and piracy whether or not anyone is watching.
- **Branding throughout** — title and loading screens, an unbroken musical theme, filtered chat, in-game guide,
  wallet and journal tabs.

## ◆ &nbsp;What is deliberately not in this build

These are designed but not implemented. They are listed so nobody installs this expecting them:

- **Romance and affection**, which the conversation system is built for and does not yet carry.
- **Weapon rarity and reforging** — the mod is installed, the ladder is not configured.
- **Trainers, teachers and respec.**
- **Faction conflict, treaties and court politics.**
- **Capturing and re-helming another ship**, and airships.
- The **Surazan dust-storm rhythm**.
- **World pregeneration and an update policy.**

Of 62 tracked systems, **48 are built and wired and 14 are partial**. That is the engineering;
the content inside it is thinner than the list above makes it sound.


## ◆ &nbsp;Changed in this build

- **The aerial look at a settlement was rebuilt.** It now begins and ends at your own eyes with no cut, rises,
  crosses the place and comes back down in one continuous movement, and it starts **when you cross the
  settlement's edge** rather than when you reach its middle — so it happens on arriving.
- **Gates you could not walk through.** Three of Southport's six gatehouses were blocked on the inside: one had
  four blocks of bank filling its gateway. The way a gatehouse takes its height, the grading of its approaches
  and a hole in the grading that left single columns standing were all fixed, and the audit that checks this now
  walks gatehouses as well as posterns — it had been reading two of eight ways in.
- **The version on the main menu** had been wrong for about fifty builds. It is checked now.

## ◆ &nbsp;Known faults in this build

Found and not yet fixed at the time of packaging:

- **One gate at Southport is still blocked**, and one postern opens onto the back of a building. Both are siting
  rather than terrain, and both are recorded.
- **Interiors are thin** — a minority of building blueprints are furnished inside.
- **Combat feel and the gear ladder above iron are untuned**, and balance in general is a first draft.
- **Almost none of this has been played.** Most of what is listed above has been built and audited, not tested
  with a person in front of it.

Crashes and generation faults not on this list are worth reporting — please open an issue.

## ◆ &nbsp;Saves

**Do not get attached to a world made with this build.** Identifiers, world format and progression change between
builds, and no migration is provided or planned during alpha.
