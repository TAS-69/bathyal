## ◆ &nbsp;Installing

Import the `.mrpack` into a launcher that supports the Modrinth format: Prism Launcher, the Modrinth App or
ATLauncher. The first import downloads most mods from their authors' own pages and can take several minutes.

Use Java 21. Allocating 8 GB is a sensible starting point. Distant Horizons is the largest default performance cost;
reduce its radius first on slower systems.

## ◆ &nbsp;What changed

- Craft professions now offer paid, staged apprenticeships and costly discipline respecialisation.
- Inns provide paid rooms, rest and a local return point. Regional transport and mount services have been accepted in
  play.
- Regional foods, preservation recipes, drinks and alchemical products now form a broader survival chain.
- Mining and Excavation have distinct exclusive paths, and custom metal tools and armour now render correctly both in
  hand and when worn.
- Combat is faster and rewards timing and positioning through precise, rear and aerial attacks and narrow perfect
  guards. Magic has clearer school-specific audiovisual feedback.
- Non-lethal crime calls the watch without making the whole settlement attack immediately; murder remains an immediate
  armed response. Markets now react to stock and demand and enforce a resale ceiling.
- Merchant and pirate shipping, crews, port turnaround, boarding and cannon combat received substantial navigation and
  behaviour work.
- Settlement discovery, map markers, aerial reveals, regional music and character creation presentation passed the
  current client test suite.
- v0.10.91 moves crafting refusals beside the crafting interface, repairs a progression reveal path and lets fixed shop
  proprietors witness visible theft. These three focused repairs still need final in-game confirmation.

## ◆ &nbsp;Current state

The public audit tracks **85 system groups: 58 implemented, 18 partial and 9 not implemented**. Distribution and full
quest-graph acceptance are deferred at this stage. Balance values remain provisional throughout the alpha.

The maintained status, verification record, remaining polish and possible future systems are in the
**[public roadmap](https://github.com/TAS-69/bathyal/blob/main/ROADMAP.md)**.

## ◆ &nbsp;Package contents

- **109 mods**; most resolve from their authors' pages during import.
- Bathyal's configuration, scripting, custom world generator, settlement system, progression, economy, law, quests,
  vessels, UI, music, ambience and voice assets.
- The Excalibur resource pack by Matt Dillow (*Maffhew*), carried unaltered and credited in full.
- Nostalgia v5.1 shader support, off by default.

Every included project, licence and source page is listed in
**[CREDITS.md](https://github.com/TAS-69/bathyal/blob/main/CREDITS.md)**. Bathyal's use of AI across code and creative
production is described in **[AI-DISCLOSURE.md](https://github.com/TAS-69/bathyal/blob/main/AI-DISCLOSURE.md)**.

## ◆ &nbsp;Known limitations

- Rare vessel collisions have been reported but have not yet been reduced to a repeatable location.
- Food/alchemy pacing, advanced equipment identity, boss balance and the wider campaign economy need more playtesting.
- Quest content exists at scale but has not completed a full end-to-end acceptance run.
- Relationships, systemic faction conflict, ship capture, airships, some regional hazards and long-term world-update
  support are unfinished.
