## ◆ &nbsp;Installing

Import the `.mrpack` into any launcher that reads the Modrinth format — **Prism Launcher**
(*Add Instance → Import → Modrinth pack*), the **Modrinth App** (*Import → From file*), or **ATLauncher**.

The first import needs an internet connection and a few minutes: **106 of the 110 mods are downloaded from their
authors' own pages** at that point rather than being carried in the file. The first world load is also slow — the
terrain is read from image data rather than generated from noise.

**8 GB** allocated is a sensible starting point. **Distant Horizons ships at a 64-chunk radius** and is by a wide
margin the heaviest thing in the pack; turn it down first if you struggle.

## ◆ &nbsp;What the pack itself adds

Everything below is this project's own code and content, not a mod someone else wrote:

- **A layered map-driven world** — a custom generator combines terrain, biome, climate and coastline data;
  ~12.3 km across, sea at Y132.
- **40 settlements** with culture-specific architecture, furnished interiors, walls, gatehouses,
  farmland and **121 roads**; **21 harbours** with working port buildings ashore.
- **Six playable races** with traits, abilities and their own learning rates, chosen at character creation.
- **26 skill trees** levelled by doing the work, with exclusive forks so two players in a trade diverge.
- **Three schools of magic**, studied rather than bought, with their own grimoires and arts.
- **Standing in three layers** — faction, settlement and personal — driving prices, greetings and available work.
- **335 quests**, conversation that varies by faction, mood and race, and local rumours.
- **A currency, shops with daily stock, and private trade** with eligible residents.
- **Crime and consequence** — stealth, theft, witnesses, the watch, fines and cells.
- **Ships bought from shipwrights**, crews who keep names, levels and loyalty, liveries, figureheads, and a sea
  that runs port-to-port trade and piracy whether or not anyone is watching.
- **Branding throughout** — title and loading screens, an unbroken musical theme, filtered chat, in-game guide,
  wallet and journal tabs.

## ◆ &nbsp;Unfinished systems

These remain unfinished; the roadmap distinguishes implementation from acceptance:

- **Complete relationship and romance content.**
- **The complete weapon rarity and reforging economy.**
- **Faction conflict, treaties and court politics.**
- **Expanded ship capture and airship gameplay.**
- The **Surazan dust-storm rhythm**.
- **World pregeneration and an update policy.**

Paid craft apprenticeships and costly discipline resets are implemented. Full quest playthroughs, cross-mod
progression acceptance, balance and multiplayer performance remain ongoing work.


## ◆ &nbsp;Changes since the last public build

- Regional settlements have been overhauled with varied sizes, lived interiors, civic amenities and actual
  Handcrafted furniture. Farm boundaries, doorway frames, roof supports and harbour approaches received repairs.
- Diagonal gatehouses retain a full curtain over their entrances. Tree-court grading and the central keep's
  placement were corrected; blueprint earth no longer creates unnecessary rectangular terrain pads.
- Ships received proactive route and clearance checks, closer/live target reassessment, steadier sails and cannons,
  and continued port trade and piracy outside loaded chunks.
- Ship route planning, hull/traffic checks, home/bed navigation and appearance caches were optimised. A controlled
  native comparison shows fewer long tick pauses; busy full-pack worlds still need profiling.
- The custom conversation interface is retained and polished: two-column choices, shorter paging controls, Back,
  a closer shoulder camera and temporary interaction holds. Unavailable purchases/services are grey with reasons.
- A compact sidebar tracks up to three quest titles and distances. Click a quest title in Journal to toggle it.
  Current main/character world guidance remains blue/purple; available work highlights the trade icon's silhouette.
- Theft witnessing, line of sight and hidden-player gaze were refined. Guards support conversation, bribes and
  pickpocketing; bribes improve local standing without changing personal regard.
- Installed blocks and items now use an explicit progression catalogue. Hay is gated by Farming, and starter
  gathering, legitimate purchases, quest rewards and grave recovery remain available through their own rules.
- Regional metal armour has temperature resistance. Custom ingredient, gathering and equipment visuals received
  refinements, and body/voice matching and occupation/name presentation were corrected.

## ◆ &nbsp;Verification and remaining work

Both public/development builds pass integrity and restriction checks. Native tests cover selected transactions,
conversation navigation, quest tracking, theft/LOS, registry coverage and all sixteen metal armour resistance
entries. Earlier controlled tests cover generated entrances/port routes, supported structures, sailing and fleet
updates. These are focused checks, not a completed campaign, full-client or multiplayer acceptance run.

Food/alchemy pacing, advanced material mechanics, boss balance, crowded-world performance, long voyages, portrait
likeness, relationships and full linked-quest playthroughs remain unfinished. New generation changes require a
fresh test world; existing chunks are not rebuilt by updating. Balance values remain provisional.

Progress, verification and planned/potential systems are maintained in the
**[public roadmap](https://github.com/TAS-69/bathyal/blob/main/ROADMAP.md)**.

## ◆ &nbsp;Saves

**Do not get attached to a world made with this build.** Identifiers, world format and progression change between
builds, and no migration is provided or planned during alpha.
