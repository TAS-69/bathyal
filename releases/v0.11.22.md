## ◆ &nbsp;Installing

Import the `.mrpack` into any launcher that reads the Modrinth format — **Prism Launcher**
(*Add Instance → Import → Modrinth pack*), the **Modrinth App** (*Import → From file*), or **ATLauncher**.

The first import needs an internet connection and a few minutes: **106 of the 110 mods are downloaded from their
authors' own pages** at that point rather than being carried in the file. The first world load is also slow — the
terrain is read from image data rather than generated from noise.

**8 GB** allocated is a sensible starting point. **Distant Horizons ships at a 64-chunk radius** and is by a wide
margin the heaviest thing in the pack; turn it down first if you struggle.

## ◆ &nbsp;What the pack itself adds

Everything below is this project's own code and content, not a mod someone else wrote:

- **A layered map-driven world** — a custom generator combines terrain, biome, climate and coastline data;
  ~12.3 km across, sea at Y132.
- **40 settlements** with culture-specific architecture, furnished interiors, walls, gatehouses,
  farmland and **121 roads**; **21 harbours** with working port buildings ashore.
- **Six playable races** with traits, abilities and their own learning rates, chosen at character creation.
- **25 skill trees** levelled by doing the work, with exclusive forks so two players in a trade diverge.
  Work you have not trained for is allowed but costly: three times as slow, a quarter of the yield (7% without
  the proper tool or weapon) and half the experience.
- **Three schools of magic**, studied rather than bought, with their own grimoires and arts.
- **Equipment quality on every piece**, fixed to that individual item and assigned the moment it is
  obtained — nothing to roll, activate or open. Its bonuses are drawn one at a time from **82 stats**, each
  with its own grade and its own strength, about one in eight of them a flaw rather than a boon; the best
  grades can roll **18 abilities** instead of a number. Optional reforging and salvage at a smith.
- **Standing in three layers** — faction, settlement and personal — driving prices, greetings and available work.
- **335 quests**, conversation that varies by faction, mood and race, and local rumours.
- **A currency, shops with daily stock, and private trade** with eligible residents.
- **Crime and consequence** — stealth, theft, witnesses, the watch, fines and cells.
- **Ships bought from shipwrights**, crews who keep names, levels and loyalty, liveries, figureheads, and a sea
  that runs port-to-port trade and piracy whether or not anyone is watching.
- **Branding throughout** — title and loading screens, an unbroken musical theme, filtered chat, in-game guide,
  wallet and journal tabs.

## ◆ &nbsp;Unfinished systems

These remain unfinished; the roadmap distinguishes implementation from acceptance:

- **Complete relationship and romance content.**
- **The balance of the equipment economy** — the quality system is in; its prices, drop pools and endgame
  ceilings are a first draft.
- **Faction conflict, treaties and court politics.**
- **Expanded ship capture and airship gameplay.**
- The **Surazan dust-storm rhythm**.
- **World pregeneration and an update policy.**

Paid craft apprenticeships and costly discipline resets are implemented. Full quest playthroughs, cross-mod
progression acceptance, balance and multiplayer performance remain ongoing work.


## ◆ &nbsp;Changes since the last public build

- **One character window.** The inventory key opens a single window with six tabs — **Health**, **Clothing**,
  **Inventory**, **Journal**, **Wallet** and the **Start Guide** — and **H**, **J** and **B** open it on Health,
  the Journal and the Wallet.
  - *Health* shows thirst, temperature, wetness, hunger and health, your conditions, and a figure that colours
    each wounded limb from yellow to deep red. Pick a limb and a remedy you carry, then **Apply**.
  - *Clothing* shows you dressed, every clothing slot, and what you carry that can be worn, with Wear and Swap.
  - The *Start Guide* has been rewritten for the current rules, with new pages on untrained work, clothing, injury
    and medicine, and cooking, brewing and alchemy.
- **Skill trees, sorted.** **K** opens an overview of every tree — its level, progress and any points waiting —
  and five tabs that sort the trees into Gathering, Craft, Sea & War, Magic, and Wilds & Society. Each tree can be
  dragged and zoomed, and its nodes are coloured by what you can do with them.
- **One look throughout.** Conversations, shops, the ship's books, character creation, every tooltip and
  vanilla's chests, crafting tables and furnaces now share the same dark-and-gold style. The containers come from a
  small resource pack of the project's own, *Bathyal UI*, which sits above Excalibur and can be turned off.
- **Food, preservation and alchemy.** 350 foods, drinks, brews, spirits and alchemical products with stated
  effects; eighteen regional crops that grow wild only in their own land; brews that ferment and age by themselves;
  drunkenness and hangovers.
- **Everyday clothes.** 328 garments in 55 kinds — hats to socks, belts, rings and necklaces — in each land's own
  cloth and dyes, layered on the body; four pieces of one style make an outfit with a small bonus.
- **Magic foci** that add their school's power and mana and spare strong spells their reagent, visible sigils for
  every spell cast, and Ars Nouveau spells counting towards the three schools.
- **Equipment:** regional metals carry signatures of their own, quality depends on where a piece was found, and
  stacked bonuses have a ceiling.
- **Keys no longer clash.** On first start the pack moves or switches off other mods' keys that shared one; the
  ship's books are now on **U**, the dodge roll on **Left Control**.
- **Fixes:** the wallet key also stopped a cutscene recording; G and N opened other mods' screens; the injury mod's
  hotbar figure duplicated the Health tab; text overlapped in empty lists.

## ◆ &nbsp;Verification and remaining work

Both public and development builds pass integrity and restriction checks, and the pack's own audits pass. The
character window, skill-tree screen, window style and key changes have been tested in play; the new food, clothing
and foci content has had controlled checks and still needs normal survival play, and its numbers are provisional.
New content requires a fresh test world.

Progress, verification and planned systems are maintained in the
**[public roadmap](https://github.com/TAS-69/bathyal/wiki/Roadmap)**.

## ◆ &nbsp;Saves

**Do not get attached to a world made with this build.** Identifiers, world format and progression change between
builds, and no migration is provided or planned during alpha.
