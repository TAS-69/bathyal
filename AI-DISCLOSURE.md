# AI disclosure

**Bathyal is AI-assisted throughout.** Code, writing, artwork and audio were all made with AI in the loop. This is
stated plainly so that people can decide what they want to install and support without having to infer how it was
made.

AI does not set Bathyal's purpose or creative direction. Those decisions remain with **TAS-69**.

<img src="assets/rule.svg" alt="" width="100%">

## ◆ &nbsp;Code

Nearly all of the implementation was produced with **Anthropic's Claude** and **OpenAI's Codex** in alternating
sessions. Tasks are assigned individually, and each result is reviewed before it remains in the project.

That work includes the custom mod (`BathyalGen`), scripting, the multi-layer map-driven world generator, settlement
generation, skill trees, quests and dialogue, the economy and law systems, ships and crews, build tooling, and the
automated audits that check each package.

## ◆ &nbsp;Writing and art

Bathyal's original writing and artwork use an **AI-assisted creative workflow**. That includes dialogue and quest
writing, prologue plates, race portraits, UI elements, item and block textures, world-map treatments, and the
promotional graphics on these pages. Output is selected, rewritten, composed, retouched and corrected before it is
integrated.

Ordinary scripts then crop, mask, recolour and composite approved source art into the sizes and frames used by the
game and these pages. The promotional graphics use screenshots of the running game, Bathyal's world map and its
city plans at their true proportions; those scripts do not repaint or extend the source images.

**The textures players see most often are third-party work and are not AI-generated for Bathyal.** The pack's
in-game visual identity is the **Excalibur** resource pack by **Matt Dillow (Maffhew)**, used unaltered and credited
in full in **[CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md)**.

## ◆ &nbsp;Audio

The music, ambience and NPC voice bank use the same **AI-assisted creative pipeline** as Bathyal's other original
content. Source material is directed and selected, then edited, remastered and integrated; the finished audio is
not presented as solely human performances or source recordings.

The final mastering is scripted: filtering, compression, fades, mono conversion and loudness matching place every
voice line at a consistent level and map it to the correct reaction — idle, greeting, trade, refusal, hurt or death.

## ◆ &nbsp;Creative direction

**TAS-69 sets the design:** what the world is, who lives in it, how it behaves, its history, races, tone, characters
and the boundaries of the project. The written brief is the canonical source. When an AI session proposes something
beyond it, that proposal is recorded as an open decision until it is accepted, changed or rejected.

**The third-party mods are their authors' work.** The third-party mods assembled by this pack are outside the workflow
described above. Each one is credited individually in **[CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md)**.

## ◆ &nbsp;How it is checked

Automated audits read generated-world data and compare it with the design:
walls, doorways, stairs, harbour access, tree clearing and structure placement, along with checks that public claims
still match the build. Hands-on playtesting supplies the other half of that process; most faults found during this
alpha came from playing the build and reporting what was wrong.

None of that makes the pack finished. See **[the alpha notice](README.md#alpha)**.
