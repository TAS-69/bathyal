# AI disclosure

**I use AI throughout this project.** Code, writing, artwork and audio were all made with AI in the loop. I state
that plainly because people should be able to decide what they want to install and support without having to infer
how it was made.

AI does not set Bathyal's purpose or creative direction. I do.

<img src="assets/rule.svg" alt="" width="100%">

## ◆ &nbsp;Code

I used **Anthropic's Claude** and **OpenAI's Codex** for nearly all of the implementation, working in alternating
sessions. I assign each task, review the result, and decide what remains in the project.

That work includes the custom mod (`BathyalGen`), scripting, the multi-layer map-driven world generator, settlement
generation, skill trees, quests and dialogue, the economy and law systems, ships and crews, build tooling, and the
automated audits that check each package.

## ◆ &nbsp;Writing and art

Bathyal's original writing and artwork use an **AI-assisted creative workflow** under my direction. That includes
dialogue and quest writing, prologue plates, race portraits, UI elements, item and block textures, world-map
treatments, and the promotional graphics on these pages. I select, rewrite, compose, retouch and correct the output
before integrating it.

Ordinary scripts then crop, mask, recolour and composite approved source art into the sizes and frames used by the
game and these pages. The promotional graphics use screenshots of the running game, Bathyal's world map and its
city plans at their true proportions; those scripts do not repaint or extend the source images.

**The textures players see most often are not mine and are not AI-generated for Bathyal.** The pack's in-game
visual identity is the **Excalibur** resource pack by **Matt Dillow (Maffhew)**, used unaltered and credited in full
in **[CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md)**.

## ◆ &nbsp;Audio

I create the music, ambience and NPC voice bank through the same **AI-assisted creative pipeline** as Bathyal's
other original content. I direct and select the source material, then edit, remaster and integrate it; I do not
present the finished audio as solely human performances or source recordings.

The final mastering is scripted: filtering, compression, fades, mono conversion and loudness matching place every
voice line at a consistent level and map it to the correct reaction — idle, greeting, trade, refusal, hurt or death.

## ◆ &nbsp;Creative direction

**I set the design.** I decide what the world is, who lives in it, how it behaves, its history, races, tone,
characters and the boundaries of the project. My written brief is the canonical source. When an AI session proposes
something beyond it, that proposal is recorded as an open decision until I accept, change or reject it.

**The third-party mods are their authors' work.** The 109 mods assembled by this pack are outside the workflow
described above. I credit each one individually in **[CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md)**.

## ◆ &nbsp;How I check it

I gate every package with automated audits that read the generated world from disk and compare it with the design:
walls, doorways, stairs, harbour access, tree clearing and structure placement, along with checks that public claims
still match the build. I also play the build and report what is wrong; most faults found during this alpha came from
that hands-on testing.

None of that makes the pack finished. See **[the alpha notice](README.md#alpha)**.
